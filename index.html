A private training and health log for Android

KD Barbell keeps your lifts, cardio, body measurements, nutrition, sleep, vitals, lab results and recovery notes in one place, on your phone and in your own private account space, and nowhere else.

You sign in with a Google account. Your records are stored in a database on your phone and synced to your own space in the app's cloud project so that the same account can restore them on another phone. Nothing is shared with other users, sold, or used for advertising. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software, and it reads Health Connect only for the categories you turn on; it never writes to Health Connect.
