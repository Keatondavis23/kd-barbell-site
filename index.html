A private training and fitness log for Android

KD Barbell keeps your lifts, cardio, body measurements, nutrition, sleep, daily check-ins and recovery notes in one place, on your phone and — if you choose to sign in — in your own private account space, and nowhere else.

In version 0.41 and later, an account is optional. Without one, your records live in a database on your phone and nowhere else — the app says Saved on this phone, and a file export is your backup. Sign in with a Google account, or with an email address and a password, and your records are synced to your own space in the app's cloud project so that the same account can restore them on another phone; signing in after using the app without an account asks you, once, whether the records on the phone stay there or are copied into the account. Nothing is shared with other users, sold, or used for advertising. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software, and it reads Health Connect only for the categories you turn on; it never writes to Health Connect.
