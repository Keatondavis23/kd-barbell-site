Reporting a problem

The more of the following you include, the faster we can help:

  1. What you did, what you expected and what happened, step by step, including the screen you were on.
  2. The app version — shown under Profile › About.
  3. The troubleshooting summary — in the app, open Profile › Account & sync › Help › Troubleshooting and tap Copy troubleshooting summary, then paste it into your email. It contains the app version, your phone's Android version, and the sync and Health Connect status with sanitized error codes. It contains no email address, identifier, record or health value, so it is safe to send.
  4. Your phone model, if the summary does not name it.

Please do not send health records. Describe the problem rather than attaching exports or screenshots that show your measurements, nutrition or other personal values. If a screenshot is needed, crop it to the part that shows the problem.

Common questions

Sign-in fails or keeps asking

KD Barbell signs in with a Google account (through Google Play services) or with an email address and password. Check that the phone has an internet connection. For Google, check that Google Play services is up to date and that the account works in other Google apps. For email and password, the app shows the same sentence for a wrong password and for an address that has no account; use Forgot password? to receive a reset link (it is sent only if an account exists), or sign in with Google if that is how the account was created and add a password afterwards under Account & sync › Sign-in. If the app says the cloud refused the account, email us with the troubleshooting summary.

No verification email

A new email account must open the verification link once. Check the spam folder, then use Resend the email on the verification screen (it waits half a minute between sends). The sender is Firebase, on behalf of KD Barbell. Once verified, signing in never asks again. If the address was mistyped, sign out and create the account again with the right one; the mistyped account can be deleted on request.

Health Connect shows nothing

Health Connect data appears only after you connect it under Profile › Data sources, turn on the categories you want and grant the Android permissions. Check that the source app (for example a watch app or Cronometer) is itself writing to Health Connect; KD Barbell only reads what is there. The Data sources screen shows what was last read and which apps are writing.

Records are missing on a second phone

Both phones must be signed in with the same KD Barbell account (Google or email and password) and have synced since the records were made. Account & sync › Sync now runs a sync immediately and shows anything waiting or any error.

Installing or updating

KD Barbell runs on Android 8.0 or newer on 64-bit phones. A build downloaded directly as an APK and a build installed from Google Play are the same app with the same signing key, so one updates the other in place.

Getting your data out

Profile › Export data writes a complete copy of your records as a file wherever you choose, either as the app's own backup file or as a ZIP of CSV spreadsheets.

Deleting your account or data

See Delete your account. Deletion requests sent by email are handled by a person and confirmed by email. We never ask for your password.
